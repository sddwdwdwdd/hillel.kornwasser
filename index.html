About

Meet Hillel

  • 2M+people reached worldwide
  • Age 13when he made his October 7th documentary

Hillel Kornwasser is a 16-year-old Israel activist, political commentator, and host of The Voice of Us on Jewish Breaking News, reaching over 2 million people worldwide.

At just 13, he created a documentary telling the story of Israel and October 7th through the eyes of a teenager, bringing a powerful and unique perspective to global conversations.

His work has taken him from rallies and campuses across the country, where he has interviewed prominent leaders and engaged with policymakers on the future of western civilization in America.

A compelling speaker, his work is driven by a deep commitment to inspire audiences of all ages and remind the world that the future depends on empowering its youth to lead.
